## 4. What this tells us (patterns, each seen in the data above)
1. **People and process beat the product.** Packing rush, the team, the kitchen, workshops: the top posts of SMOOR,
   Bombay Sweet Shop and ChocoMania itself. A finished cake alone is her weakest post.
2. **Eggless is a reach engine.** Eggless recipes and "you don't need eggs" posts reach hundreds of thousands. ChocoMania
   is eggless by default: short eggless tips / mini recipes can reach non-followers (Trial Reels), the order posts sell.
3. **Festive rush is the best brand moment.** Both big brands' best reels are from Diwali packing week. Next: Christmas,
   New Year corporate boxes, wedding season (now to Feb), Valentine's, Rakhi, Diwali 2027 (start in September).
4. **Every selling post ends in one action.** "DM for orders", phone number, "comment X". Local winners all do it.
5. **Consistency.** The big brands post 6-7 times a week, 3 in 4 are reels. ChocoMania went 4 months without a post.
6. **A novelty concept travels.** "Maggi cake" style ideas get tagged and shared (shares = reach on Instagram).

## 5. What NOT to do
- No bare cake photo with a hashtag wall and no hook (local pages at 20-50 likes).
- No empty captions; no 4-month gaps.
- No made-up prices, dates or claims: prices, workshop dates and delivery area come from Teena only.
- No AI-made pictures of her products: only her real photos and videos (brand trust; same rule as VAJRA).
- No copying another baker's photos or recipes.
