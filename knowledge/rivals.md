# Competitors and brands: weekly read (2026-11-18, engine/rivals.py --weekly)

## tech
What the leaders do that we do not:
- Hafu Go: High-concept visual experiments/hacks (Coke slushie, ice hammer) with 'I Tested' personal POV
- Professor Of How: 3D animated explainers of current events/security (Salman Khan Y+, Tiger vs Bishnoi) within 48h
- Trakin Tech: 'Best of' ranking videos with clear decision metrics (Top 5 under 50k, Best Ultra phone)
- Info Family: Pure curiosity questions answered visually (Death Shadow, Moon count, Earth rings) no fluff
- Science to Technology: Engineering debunking with hands-on demo (Microcontroller in torch = dumb)
Proven topics for us:
- Pressure cooker ka whistle kaise kaam karta hai? Ek visual metaphor mein
- Main aaj test karunga: kya phone ko microwave mein daalne se charge hota hai?
- Naya Parliament building 3D mein: security features kaise kaam karte hain?
- Sabse best budget phone 2024: yeh 3 metrics decide karenge
- Wireless charger ka coil asli mein kaise kaam karta hai - visual proof
Gap to own: Nobody does 'one curiosity question + one visual metaphor + speech from frame 0' in 30-60s consistently. The gap is ultra-short, metaphor-driven explainers without intro/outro fluff.
Brands in this niche: Smartphone brands (Samsung, Xiaomi, OnePlus), Chip makers (Qualcomm, MediaTek), Accessory brands (Anker, Baseus), Ed-tech (PhysicsWallah, Unacademy), Telecom (Jio, Airtel)
Why a brand would pick us: We offer focused, metaphor-driven explainers that simplify complex tech for mass Hindi audience in <60s. Must build: consistent visual metaphor library and 3D/animation pipeline.

## katha
What the leaders do that we do not:
- Tilak: Musical/devotional format with poetic lyrics (Sita-Ram bhajans) not storytelling
- The Divine Tales: Festival-specific ritual explanations (Navratri jau) tied to calendar
- Bhakti Gyan: Vrat/fasting kathas with year/date in title (Purnima 2023, Nirjala Ekadashi)
- Rahasya Duniya: Mystery/historical proof angles (Ancient treasure tech, Ramayan evidence)
- The Divine Journey: Mystical/controversial saint stories (Neem Karoli Baba death foresight)
Proven topics for us:
- Mahabharat, Adi Parva, Adhyay 62: Ekalavya ne angushtha kyun kaata? Ek lesson guru-bhakti ka
- Shiv Puran, Rudra Samhita: Somvar vrat katha - kyun chandra dev ko shraap mila?
- Valmiki Ramayan, Yuddha Kand, Sarg 127: Vibhishan ne Ravan ko kyun chhoda? Nishchay ka ek niyam
- Bhagwat Puran, Dasham Skandh, Adhyay 10: Govardhan puja ka asli raaz - prakriti vs ahankar
- Padma Puran, Srishti Khanda: Neem Karoli Baba ne apni mrityu kaise jaani? Shraddha ka ek siddhant
Gap to own: Nobody does 'one story + one clear lesson + explicit source citation (text + chapter/kand)' in 45-75s Shorts format. Most are long-form or devotional music.
Brands in this niche: Puja items (Cycle Pure Agarbatti, Mangaldeep), Ayurvedic (Patanjali, Dabur), Spiritual apps (Sadhguru, Art of Living), Festive wear (Manyavar), Donation platforms (Ketto, GiveIndia)
Why a brand would pick us: We deliver scripture-verified, lesson-driven micro-kathas that respect tradition while fitting Shorts attention spans. Must build: searchable scripture database and lesson-extraction framework.
