## What we sell
Daily Instagram + Facebook + YouTube Shorts reels made from the business's OWN photos/videos, posted on schedule,
with captions and hashtags. Proof: VAJRA (engine/biz.py, businesses/vajra) runs on it every day.

## Market price (checked 20 Nov)
- Indian social media management quotes run from about Rs 8,000 to Rs 1.5 lakh a month
  ([Codezion 2026 guide](https://www.codezion.com/blog/social-media-marketing-cost-in-india-the-complete-2026-pricing-guide/)).
- Our cost per business is near Rs 0 (free models, own server), so we can sit at the low end and still earn.

## Who to pitch first
Local businesses with good photos but weak reels: interior / furniture / construction material (like VAJRA),
cafes and restaurants, gyms, salons, coaching classes, real estate agents. Source list: public Google Maps /
Instagram business profiles (engine/hookstudy.py Business Discovery can read follower counts and last post date).
Target = business with >= 20 posts but no reel in the last 30 days.
