## Editing that wins
**Core Pattern: Evidence-First Editing. If a tool, document, or mechanism is mentioned, it must be visually dissected within 500ms.**

*   **Physical Grounding:** Holding a physical document or object (Paper Form VRnKIWK4tmo 18.03x, PS5 Box p_N-jF8IkJ8 3.3x) builds instant trust compared to pure digital assets.
*   **The 3D Mystery:** Use high-fidelity 3D cross-sections to reveal "hidden" mechanics (Veritasium oR94cicO7xM 5.12x, CleoAbram kCD532ylVGc 4.11x).
*   **Split-Screen Authority:** Maintain a narrator in a bottom PiP while the top half shows real-time news or UI navigation (VarunMayya dmSAVk8vwTw 8.44x, biAwtOHBve8 4.05x).
*   **Yellow Highlight Proof:** Use yellow highlights on key words in documents or UI to guide the eye (SarkariDNA VRnKIWK4tmo 18.03x, TheAIUniversity jLRIxgyr2hs 22.99x).
*   **Satisfying Stacking:** Use repetitive, stacking animations for historical or list-based content (Fireship aXcuz6fn8_w 3.47x).

*   **Visual Change Rate:** Aim for 1.5s–3.0s per shot (GyanTherapy qamB6ygkQR8 3.0x at 1.7s; VarunMayya dmSAVk8vwTw 8.44x at 1.75s).

*   **Captions:** 2–4 words per chunk, centered. Use word-by-word highlighting (RajShamani Q0KpivgwztQ 8.45x).

## What flops
1. **The "Ad-Vibe" Bait:** Transitioning from a personal hook into a sponsored tutorial kills retention (dhruvrathee fHU4neQCpkc 0.18x, aevytv XSXv0JLHp7Y 0.24x).
2. **Tutorial Drag:** Spending >30s on a technical "how-to" without escalating stakes (VarunMayya OlFMwxTI3bk 0.18x).
3. **No-Context Clips:** Visuals without a narrative or educational explanation fail (mkbhd M4xlXvCAc2E 0.24x).
4. **Generic Montages:** Silent patriotic or aesthetic montages fail in this niche (SarkariDNA oGm1OyVV1hw 0.25x).
5. **Dry Spec Recitation:** Listing specs without a narrative hook or music feels flat (TechnicalGuruji L_IDWnPsNOc 0.3x).
