## Editing that wins
**Core Pattern: Evidence-first editing. If you name a tool, show the GitHub repo or UI immediately.**

### A) Visual Anchors & "Maps"
- **The Diagram Backbone:** Keep a complex architectural diagram on screen as a "map," only animating the specific nodes being discussed (551lh10g_go 4.4x, S4tLX3kq4Ik 3.74x, x8lcdDbKZto 2.86x).
- **Recurring Physical Objects:** Use a specific product (Mercedes EQBLRjWmNBk 36x, Remote dX_fna-Bx4E 5.0x) or a 3D model (King Tut tomb kCD532ylVGc 4.11x) to ground the narrative.

### B) Evidence Overlays
- **UI/Repo Proof:** Show real GitHub repositories, code snippets, or news articles with yellow highlights on 1-2 key words (zsah85KcyTQ 7.08x, biAwtOHBve8 4.05x, vzO_Tc9AQ10 4.57x).
- **Split-Screen/PiP:** Use a split-screen with the creator/narrator at the bottom and graphics/evidence on top to maintain a "human" connection while showing data (vzO_Tc9AQ10 4.57x, biAwtOHBve8 4.05x, S4tLX3kq4Ik 3.74x).

### C) Satisfying Patterns
- **Stacking Animations:** Use repetitive visual logic, like logos dropping into a stack, to create rhythm (aXcuz6fn8_w 3.47x).
- **Listicle Structure:** Use large on-screen numbers (2, 3, 5) to signal progress through a list (vzO_Tc9AQ10 4.57x, x8lcdDbKZto 2.86x).

## Sound
- **Voice:** High-energy, fast-paced delivery is standard (EQBLRjWmNBk 36x, dX_fna-Bx4E 5.0x). AI voices must be professional and clear (zsah85KcyTQ 7.08x).
- **Music:** Use subtle tech/upbeat beds (2KmY_lSQi10 4.07x, zsah85KcyTQ 7.08x). **Exception:** Pure technical explainers can succeed with **zero music** if the visual information density is high (551lh10g_go 4.4x).
- **SFX:** Use "whooshes" for transitions and "clicks/pops" for UI interactions (biAwtOHBve8 4.05x, dX_fna-Bx4E 5.0x, y1bUUkIvPQE 2.66x).

## Pacing numbers
- **Visual Change Rate:** Aim for 1.5s–3.0s per shot for general tech/vlog styles (EQBLRjWmNBk 36x at 1.5s, dX_fna-Bx4E 5.0x at 1.67s). Diagrams can stay longer (5s+) if they are being actively annotated (S4tLX3kq4Ik 3.74x).
- **Spoken Density:** Target 2.4–2.8 words per second (EQBLRjWmNBk 36x at 2.8, vzO_Tc9AQ10 4.57x at 2.8). Avoid dropping below 2.0 wps (GycFYrtbVqw 0.14x flopped at 0.8 wps).
- **Captions:** 3–6 words per chunk, centered, with yellow/green highlights on key nouns (biAwtOHBve8 4.05x, zsah85KcyTQ 7.08x).

## What flops
1. **The "Ad" Vibe:** Sponsored content that feels like a manual/instructional guide rather than a story (ByvdT-CDfoE 0.15x, fHU4neQCpkc 0.18x, 5bFS4wIl4W0 0.2x).
2. **Tutorial Drag:** Transitioning from a cool hook into a slow, 60-second technical "how-to" kills retention (OlFMwxTI3bk 0.18x).
3. **Repetitive Loops:** Visuals that repeat without escalating the narrative stake (GycFYrtbVqw 0.14x).
4. **Missing Captions:** High-quality 3D visuals fail if there are no captions to anchor the audio (GycFYrtbVqw 0.14x).
5. **Safety Triggers:** Visceral or "gross-out" medical content (EHhqfU1kXxQ 0.13x).

## Never
- Glows/neon, particle bursts, crossfades between scenes, bouncy easing, text or labels in the corners, HUD counters, stat slides, slide/deck layouts, stock-template looks, idle floating/breathing loops, dead time >1 s without a reason, anything appearing before it is spoken, invented facts or numbers, mock UI where a real screenshot exists.
