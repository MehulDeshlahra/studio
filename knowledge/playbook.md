## Editing that wins
**Core Pattern: Evidence-First Editing. If a tool or document is mentioned, it must be on screen within 500ms.**

### A) Visual Anchors & "Maps"
*   **The Diagram Backbone:** Use a single, complex diagram as the visual base, animating only the nodes being discussed (ByteByteGo S4tLX3kq4Ik 3.74x, Yj9qOWnipTQ 2.18x). This keeps the viewer oriented during technical explanations.
*   **Physical Evidence:** Show real paper documents or hardware. Holding a physical object (Remote dX_fna-Bx4E 5.0x, PS5 Box p_N-jF8IkJ8 3.3x, Paper Form VRnKIWK4tmo 18.03x) grounds the narrative in reality.
*   **The "Skeptic vs. Expert" Split:** Use a back-and-forth dialogue format to build tension and resolve it with proof (ThinkSchool lP4enqLb-uM 2.9x).

### B) Evidence Overlays
*   **Split-Screen UI:** Keep the narrator in a bottom PiP/Split-screen while the top half shows real-time UI navigation, news articles, or chip reveals (VarunMayya dmSAVk8vwTw 8.44x, biAwtOHBve8 4.05x, SC6_oZ24FRM 3.23x).
*   **Yellow Highlight Proof:** When showing screenshots, use yellow highlights or red boxes on key words/buttons to guide the eye (VarunMayya biAwtOHBve8 4.05x, SarkariDNA QNd3SjbXb9o 8.2x, VRnKIWK4tmo 18.03x).
*   **Progressive Reveal:** Use a list or progress bar to track points (TheAIUniversity I3g_mL4LrWs 2.58x, TechWiser GiDHBsBRDro 2.37x).

### C) Satisfying Patterns
*   **Stacking Animations:** Use repetitive visual logic, like logos dropping into a stack, to create rhythm (Fireship aXcuz6fn8_w 3.47x).
*   **Visual Stunts:** Use high-effort visual sequences (iPhone Dominoes CxLCWY4K2i4 2.61x) or mechanical "wow" factors (Pop-up camera aJ7dEmaoK3Q 3.73x) to maintain retention.

## Pacing numbers
*   **Visual Change Rate:** Aim for 1.5s–3.0s per shot (IshanSharma7390 EQBLRjWmNBk 36.0x at 1.5s, TechnicalGuruji aJ7dEmaoK3Q 3.73x at 2.0s). Avoid shots longer than 5s unless it is an evolving diagram (ByteByteGo Yj9qOWnipTQ 2.18x).
*   **Spoken Density:** Target 2.5–3.5 words per second (IshanSharma7390 EQBLRjWmNBk 36.0x at 2.8, TechWiser otwhI0lg72c 5.65x). Flops occur when density drops below 1.0 wps (zackdfilms GycFYrtbVqw 0.14x at 0.8 wps).
*   **Captions:** 3–6 words per chunk, centered. Use yellow highlights for key nouns (TheAIUniversity jLRIxgyr2hs 22.99x, VarunMayya biAwtOHBve8 4.05x, CleoAbram kCD532ylVGc 4.11x).

## What flops
1.  **The "Ad-Vibe" Bait:** Transitioning from a personal hook into a sponsored tutorial kills retention (dhruvrathee fHU4neQCpkc 0.18x, aevytv XSXv0JLHp7Y 0.24x).
2.  **Visceral/Gross Content:** Medical or "gross-out" content triggers safety filters (zackdfilms EHhqfU1kXxQ 0.13x, GycFYrtbVqw 0.14x).
3.  **Tutorial Drag:** Spending >30s on a technical "how-to" without escalating stakes (VarunMayya OlFMwxTI3bk 0.18x).
4.  **Missing Captions:** High-quality visuals fail without text anchors (zackdfilms EHhqfU1kXxQ 0.14x, SarkariDNA DkFIevn9uJg 0.28x).
5.  **Saturated Topics:** Generic advice on common topics performs poorly (IshanSharma7390 SN1pPQg-5PU 0.2x).

## Never
*   Glows/neon, particle bursts, crossfades, bouncy easing, text in corners, HUD counters, stat slides, slide/deck layouts, stock-template looks, idle breathing loops, dead time >1s, anything appearing before it is spoken, invented facts, mock UI where a real screenshot exists.
