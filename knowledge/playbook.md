## Hooks that win
- 2-4 words that interrupt: "Everybody stop." (Cleo Abram, 3.4M), "Breaking news," (Varun Mayya, 959k),
  "Wait, somebody just..." (Aevy TV, 1M).
- Make the viewer the subject or show the result at frame 0. The hook never explains itself.
- The hook line is on screen as text by frame 1 (most viewers start muted).

## Editing that wins
- Evidence editing: real article/post/repo screenshots, the exact phrase highlighted as it is spoken (Varun Mayya).
- A recurring home object the video returns to, adding one new mark each time (Cleo's 3D tomb, 8+ returns).
- Stack numbers in everyday Indian terms until the total lands (Dhruv Rathee, 23M).
- Every noun gets its picture on the word it is spoken (Kurzgesagt).
- Scenes connect through an object on screen; cut mid-motion; one direction of travel across cuts.

## Sound
- Voice first; music about 9 dB under the voice in 300 Hz-4 kHz; one sound per real event, placed by its peak.
- 2-4 short silences before the biggest reveals.
- Indian winners master loud: -11 to -13 LUFS integrated, true peak -1 dBTP.

## Pacing numbers
- New picture every 1.8-2.8 s (winners); 3.5-3.8 spoken words a second.
- Hold a line to be read 0.5 s + 0.3 s a word; the last line at least 2 s.

## Never
Glows/neon, particle bursts, crossfades between scenes, bouncy easing, text or labels in the corners, HUD
counters, stat slides, slide/deck layouts, stock-template looks, idle floating/breathing loops, dead time over
1 s without a reason, anything appearing before it is spoken, invented facts or numbers, mock UI where a real
screenshot exists.
