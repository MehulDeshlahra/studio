## Updated
Updated (Nov 2026): Performance analysis of 30 Shorts confirms that high-utility, evidence-first content dominates. Winners prioritize **high-stakes hooks + immediate visual proof (UI/GitHub/Diagrams) + high spoken density**. Utility-based content (e.g., government portals, tech hacks) shows massive scaling potential (SarkariDNA 18.03x, 8.2x). Flops are defined by "Ad-vibe" (sponsored content that feels like a manual) and slow pacing (dhruvrathee 0.15x, IshanSharma7390 0.2x).

## Hooks that win
**Winners open with a bold claim, an interruption, or a vulnerability that creates an immediate "open loop."**

1. **The "Vulnerability/Confession" Hook**
- “I have to admit something.” (TheAIUniversity 22.99x)

2. **The "Urgent Interruption" Hook**
- “Everybody stop!” (CleoAbram 4.11x)
- “Shocking news.” (VarunMayya 4.05x, 8.44x)

3. **The "Contrarian/Brand Challenge" Hook**
- “Whatever Apple does, Android does it better.” (TechWiser 5.65x)
- “AI is not coming for your job.” (TheAIUniversity 2.62x)
- “Vibe coding just reached your TV.” (AIAnytime 7.08x)

4. **The "Aspirational/High-Stakes" Hook**
- “Guys, money will come and go.” (IshanSharma7390 36x)
- “Every house should have this gadget!” (TechWiser 5.0x)
- "SIR ki final voter list aa gayi hai" (SarkariDNA 8.2x)

## Editing that wins
**Core Pattern: Evidence-first editing. If you name a tool, show the GitHub repo or UI immediately.**

### A) Visual Anchors & "Maps"
- **The Diagram Backbone:** Keep a complex architectural diagram on screen as a "map," only animating the specific nodes being discussed (ByteByteGo 4.4x, 3.74x).
- **Recurring Physical Objects:** Use a specific product (Mercedes EQBLRjWmNBk 36x, Remote dX_fna-Bx4E 5.0x) or a 3D model (King Tut tomb kCD532ylVGc 4.11x) to ground the narrative.

### B) Evidence Overlays
- **UI/Repo Proof:** Show real GitHub repositories, code snippets, or news articles with yellow highlights on 1-2 key words (AIAnytime 7.08x, VarunMayya 4.05x, 4.57x).
- **Split-Screen/PiP:** Use a split-screen with the creator/narrator at the bottom and graphics/evidence on top to maintain a "human" connection while showing data (VarunMayya 4.57x, 4.05x, ByteByteGo 3.74x).

### C) Satisfying Patterns
- **Stacking Animations:** Use repetitive visual logic, like logos dropping into a stack, to create rhythm (Fireship 3.47x).
- **Listicle Structure:** Use large on-screen numbers (2, 3, 5) to signal progress (VarunMayya 4.57x).

## Sound
- **Voice:** High-energy, fast-paced delivery is standard (IshanSharma7390 36x, TechWiser 5.0x). AI voices must be professional and clear (AIAnytime 7.08x).
- **Music:** Use subtle tech/upbeat beds (aevytv 4.07x, AIAnytime 7.08x). **Exception:** Pure technical explainers can succeed with **zero music** if visual density is high (ByteByteGo 4.4x, SarkariDNA 8.2x).
- **SFX:** Use "whooshes" for transitions and "clicks/pops" for UI interactions (VarunMayya 4.05x, TechWiser 5.0x).

## Pacing numbers
- **Visual Change Rate:** Aim for 1.5s–3.0s per shot for general tech/vlog styles (IshanSharma7390 36x at 1.5s, TechWiser 5.0x at 1.67s). Diagrams can stay longer (5s+) if actively annotated (ByteByteGo 3.74x).
- **Spoken Density:** Target 2.4–2.8 words per second (IshanSharma7390 36x at 2.8, VarunMayya 4.57x at 2.8). Avoid dropping below 2.0 wps (zackdfilms 0.14x flopped at 0.8 wps).
- **Captions:** 3–6 words per chunk, centered, with yellow/green highlights on key nouns (VarunMayya 4.05x, AIAnytime 7.08x).

## What flops
1. **The "Ad" Vibe:** Sponsored content that feels like a manual rather than a story (IshanSharma7390 0.2x, dhruvrathee 0.18x).
2. **Tutorial Drag:** Transitioning from a cool hook into a slow, 60-second technical "how-to" kills retention (VarunMayya 0.18x).
3. **Repetitive Loops:** Visuals that repeat without escalating the narrative stake (zackdfilms 0.14x).
4. **Missing Captions:** High-quality visuals fail if there are no captions to anchor the audio (zackdfilms 0.14x, SarkariDNA 0.21x).
5. **Safety Triggers:** Visceral or "gross-out" medical content (zackdfilms 0.13x).

## Never
- Glows/neon, particle bursts, crossfades, bouncy easing, text in corners, HUD counters, stat slides, slide/deck layouts, stock-template looks, idle breathing loops, dead time >1s, anything appearing before it is spoken, invented facts, mock UI where a real screenshot exists.
