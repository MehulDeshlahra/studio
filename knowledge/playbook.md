## Updated
Performance analysis of 40 Shorts in late 2026 confirms that **High-Utility Evidence** and **Physical Grounding** are the primary drivers of viral growth in the Indian AI/Tech niche. Winners prioritize immediate visual proof—physical documents, real UI, or evolving diagrams—over generic B-roll. High-stakes utility (SarkariDNA 18.03x, 8.2x) and personal/aspirational hooks (IshanSharma7390 36.0x, TheAIUniversity 22.99x) dominate. Flops are characterized by the "Ad-vibe" (dhruvrathee 0.15x, 0.23x), tutorial drag (VarunMayya 0.18x), or visceral content that triggers safety filters (zackdfilms 0.13x).

## Editing that wins
**Core Pattern: Evidence-First Editing. If a tool or document is mentioned, it must be on screen within 500ms.**

### A) Visual Anchors & "Maps"
*   **The Diagram Backbone:** Use a single, complex architectural diagram as the visual base, animating only the nodes being discussed (ByteByteGo 4.4x, 3.74x, 2.18x). This keeps the viewer oriented during dense technical explanations.
*   **Physical Evidence:** Show real paper documents or hardware. Holding a physical object (Remote dX_fna-Bx4E 5.0x, PS5 Box p_N-jF8IkJ8 3.3x, Paper Form VRnKIWK4tmo 18.03x) grounds the AI narrative in reality.
*   **The "Skeptic vs. Expert" Split:** Use a back-and-forth dialogue format to build tension and resolve it with proof (ThinkSchool 2.9x).

### B) Evidence Overlays
*   **Split-Screen UI:** Keep the narrator in a bottom PiP/Split-screen while the top half shows real-time UI navigation, news articles, or chip reveals (VarunMayya 8.44x, 4.57x, 3.23x).
*   **Yellow Highlight Proof:** When showing screenshots, use yellow highlights or red boxes on key words/buttons to guide the eye (VarunMayya 4.05x, SarkariDNA 8.2x, 18.03x).
*   **Progressive Reveal:** Use a list or progress bar to track points (TheAIUniversity 2.58x, TechWiser 2.37x).

### C) Satisfying Patterns
*   **Stacking Animations:** Use repetitive visual logic, like logos dropping into a stack, to create rhythm (Fireship 3.47x).
*   **Visual Stunts:** Use high-effort visual sequences (iPhone Dominoes 2.61x) or mechanical "wow" factors (Pop-up camera aJ7dEmaoK3Q 3.73x) to maintain retention.

## Sound
*   **Voice:** High-energy, fast-paced delivery is standard (IshanSharma7390 36.0x, TechWiser 5.65x). 
*   **Music:** Upbeat, tech-focused beds (aevytv 4.07x). **Exception:** Pure technical/utility explainers can succeed with **zero music** to emphasize authority (ByteByteGo 4.4x, SarkariDNA 18.03x, 8.2x).
*   **SFX:** Use "whooshes" for transitions and "clicks/pops" for UI interactions (VarunMayya 4.05x, TechWiser 5.0x, aXcuz6fn8_w 3.47x).

## Pacing numbers
*   **Visual Change Rate:** Aim for 1.5s–2.5s per shot (IshanSharma7390 36.0x at 1.5s, TechnicalGuruji 3.73x at 2.0s). Avoid shots longer than 5s unless it is an evolving diagram (ByteByteGo 3.74x).
*   **Spoken Density:** Target 2.5–2.8 words per second (IshanSharma7390 36.0x at 2.8, VarunMayya 4.57x at 2.8). Flops occur when density drops (zackdfilms 0.14x at 0.8 wps).
*   **Captions:** 3–6 words per chunk, centered. Use yellow/green highlights for key nouns (TheAIUniversity 22.99x, VarunMayya 4.57x, CleoAbram 4.11x).

## What flops
1.  **The "Ad-Vibe" Bait:** Transitioning from a news hook into a sponsored tutorial kills retention (dhruvrathee 0.15x, 0.23x, IshanSharma7390 0.2x, aevytv 0.24x).
2.  **Tutorial Drag:** Spending >30s on a technical "how-to" without escalating stakes (VarunMayya 0.18x, aevytv 0.21x).
3.  **Missing Captions:** High-quality visuals fail without text anchors (zackdfilms 0.14x, SarkariDNA 0.21x, mkbhd 0.24x).
4.  **Safety Triggers:** Visceral medical or "gross-out" content (zackdfilms 0.13x).

## Never
*   Glows/neon, particle bursts, crossfades, bouncy easing, text in corners, HUD counters, stat slides, slide/deck layouts, stock-template looks, idle breathing loops, dead time >1s, anything appearing before it is spoken, invented facts, mock UI where a real screenshot exists.
