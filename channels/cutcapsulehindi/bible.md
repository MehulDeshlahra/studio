# Cut Capsule Hindi (@cutcapsule.hindi) - clip page bible (19 Nov 2026)

Sister page of Cut Capsule (owner picked the name 19 Nov). Same clip engine (engine/clipper.py + clipcore.py on the
laptop), same look and logo; Hindi/Hinglish sources and an Indian audience.

## Why a separate page (knowledge/clipping.md, 19 Nov)
- Global campaigns (Content Rewards, Vyro) pay for US/tier-1 views and do not pay India/Pakistan/Bangladesh views;
  several need 50-70% US audience. A mixed Hindi + English page fails that, so Cut Capsule stays English/US.
- Indian clipping platforms pay Indian views in rupees to UPI: ClipConnect (Rs 10-100 per 1K, 10% fee), Dashrize
  (weekly), INDclipping (Rs 30-100 per 1K), Klipp.in, Vyuzz. This page is their account.

## Hard rules
- Post only with permission: a source in channels/cutcapsulehindi/sources.json (a joined campaign or the creator's OK).
- Never cross-post a clip that went to Cut Capsule, and never an English source.
- Follow every brief exactly; if a brief bans something (AI voice, music, other footage), never do it.
- Never betting, casino or real-money games: India's Online Gaming Act 2025 makes advertising them a crime.
- Caption credits the creator. No on-screen AI label.

## Format
- Same as Cut Capsule (see channels/cutcapsule/bible.md): per-shot layout, hook banner, 3-word pop captions,
  30-45 s, logo top-left.
- Hook titles and captions in Hinglish, Latin script ("Isne interview mein sach bol diya").
- Posting: Indian slots (13:00 and 20:00 IST from engine/publish.py), warm-up from 21 Nov.
