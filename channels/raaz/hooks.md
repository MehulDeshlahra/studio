# What the top faceless raaz Shorts do in the first 2 seconds (2026-11-18, 6 videos watched)

Top performers (48M combined) use a split-screen 3D animation + presenter template with a ≤1 s cut, immediate news-tone VO, and a proper-noun-first hook. All six winners open with audio at frame 0, ≤5-word text on screen, and a specific, high-stakes claim — never a greeting.

## Rules for our first 2 seconds (obey)
- First audio (voiceover, SFX, or music) starts at frame 0 — zero silence.
- On-screen text appears at frame 0, ≤ 5 words, high-contrast, center or top-third.
- First spoken word is a proper noun (name, place, date) or 'The victim's' — no greetings, no 'hello', no 'aaj'.
- First visual is either a split-screen (3D animation top + presenter bottom) OR a single high-stakes image (hospital, street, presenter close-up) — no generic b-roll.
- First cut ≤ 2.0 s for videos targeting > 8 M views; ≤ 4.0 s otherwise.
- Hook promises a specific, verifiable claim (celebrity conflict, crime detail, conspiracy date) within the first spoken sentence.
- Presenter (if visible) makes direct eye contact, neutral-to-serious expression, no smiling.
- Background audio matches tone: news-style VO for celebrity/crime, ominous music for mystery, distress SFX for victim stories.
- No lower-thirds, watermarks, or channel branding in frames 0–60.

## Never
- Generic greetings or channel intros ('namaste dosto', 'welcome back')
- Silent or music-only starts without voice/SFX at frame 0
- First cut after 4 s unless the single shot is a high-stakes visual with immediate distress audio
- On-screen text longer than 5 words or appearing after frame 0
- Smiling presenter, casual posture, or busy background in the cold open
