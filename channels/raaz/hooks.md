- On-screen text ≤ 5 words, appears at frame 0, high-contrast, all-caps or large Hindi font.

- Motion or visible change begins by frame 0 (hand movement, zoom, cut, animation) — no frozen static unless shock rule applies.
- Background music is upbeat/rhythmic with a riser, or distressing vocal — no ambient/lo-fi beds.
- Frame 0 shows the stakes or game visually: injury, product, puzzle, or controversial object.

## Never
- Cold open with black screen, logo, or channel name.

- Slow zoom-ins without concurrent action or audio riser.
- Text-heavy frames (>5 words) without a clear visual anchor.
- First 2 seconds silent or with only ambient/lo-fi audio.
