## Rules for our first 2 seconds (obey)
- First spoken word is a proper noun, specific date, or direct dialogue — zero greetings, intros, or filler.
- On-screen text ≤ 5 words, appears at frame 0, reads like a headline (or is absent).
- Frame 0 visual shows the exact artifact, location, face, or setting central to the story — no generic b-roll.
- Voiceover or on-camera dialogue begins at frame 0 — no silence, no music-only lead-in.
- First hard cut occurs at or before 2.5 s (top 3 by views cut at 2 s or 1 s).
- Opening sentence contains a named entity (person, place, date) or a verbatim shocking quote.
- Emotional stake (mystery, outrage, shock, intrigue) is explicit in the first sentence.

## Never
- Greetings or channel intros ("namaste", "welcome", logo animations).
- Meta-commentary ("in this video", "today we talk about").
- Generic stock footage not tied to the specific story.
- Silence or music-only first second.
- On-screen text longer than 7 words.
