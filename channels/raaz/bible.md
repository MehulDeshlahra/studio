# raaz bible: "Raaz Ka Faisla" (@raazkafaisla, free on 16 Nov)
- Promise: a short fictional mystery where YOU decide what happens. Each case runs 3-4 parts. Every part ends on a
  choice (A or B); the comment vote picks the next part. The final part reveals the truth and who guessed right.
- Narrator: "Inspector Vikram Rathore" (voice only + seen from behind / silhouette, never a full face close-up):
  tall Indian man in his 40s, grey stubble, long dark brown coat, old steel wristwatch, always holding a small
  brass torch. Reuse this description verbatim in every prompt that shows him.
- Look: cinematic night thriller, rain, film grain, cold teal shadows with warm amber practical lights, 9:16,
  slow camera moves; locations are Indian (old havelis, railway stations, hill-station hotels, dhabas, mills).
- Voice: Gemini, low, calm, tense; short sentences; a pause before the choice.
- Structure of a part: 1 s cold open on the strangest detail -> what happened -> 2-3 clues (each clue on screen as a
  card: "SURAAG 1") -> the choice: "A: ... ya B: ... ? Comment karo A ya B." -> "Agla part kal".
- Rules: pure fiction (no real crimes, places named only generically, no real people); no gore, no violence shown;
  no suicide; fair-play clues (the answer must be findable from the clues shown).
- Never: on-screen AI label, real brand names.
