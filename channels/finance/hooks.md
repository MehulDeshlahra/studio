## Patterns that held the viewer (why_it_worked, repeated across the watched reels)
- The ₹ number or the threat is in the FIRST sentence; no intro.
- A relatable scene (my gold earrings, my stolen car, money from a stranger), then the rule.
- The maths is shown step by step on screen, big numbers, red vs green.
- One action the viewer can do today; the video is worth saving.
- 3-7 cuts per 10 s; captions with the key word highlighted.
