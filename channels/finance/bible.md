# finance bible: "Finance Fuel" (@finance.fuel; deep dive 19 Nov 2026, knowledge/new-page.md)
- Promise: one money RIGHT, TRAP or SCAM in 30-48 seconds, with the rule's real source on screen. Education, never advice.
- Audience: Indians 18-32 who just started earning (first salary, first card, first loan), Hinglish speakers.
- Why we can win (rivals.md): 25 of the 30 top rival reels we watched are a person talking to camera. Small pages
  with under 1,000 subscribers got 170k-610k Shorts views in 30 days with ONE useful rule or warning (gold purity,
  wrong-UPI-refund scam, old gold resale). Topic and hook beat channel size. Our edge is faceless authority: the real
  RBI / UPI / bank logo, the real app screen, the exact ₹ number, and a source card nobody else shows.

## Format (decided from the rival study)
- Length 30-48 s (72-118 words). The big creators run 60-120 s because people already know them; we do not yet.
- Hook in the first 4-6 words, 4 patterns, rotate (see hooks.md):
  1. Bank pays you: "UPI fail hua, paisa kata? Bank aapko roz ₹100 dega."
  2. Scam alert: "Account mein anjaan paisa aaya? Ruko. Wapas mat bhejna."
  3. Mistake warning: "Gold kharidne ja rahe ho? Ye 3 galtiyan aapka paisa kha jaati hain."
  4. Two-person money comparison: "Rahul ne phone No Cost EMI pe liya, Aditya ne cash mein. Kisne zyada diya?"
- Beats: hook (0-2 s) -> the rule or trap in one line + SOURCE CARD on screen (who set it, date) -> how it works in
  3-4 steps (flow / ui / compare scenes, numbers big) -> what YOU do (one action, e.g. "bank ko likh ke complaint do,
  phir RBI CMS portal") -> "Save karo, kaam aayega" + one question for the comments.
- On screen: big ₹ numbers (stat), red = what you lose, green = what you get (compare), phone notifications and app
  screens (ui/chat), the real logo of RBI / NPCI / the bank / the app at its first mention, 3-7 cuts per 10 s,
  captions with the key word highlighted. One source card scene in every reel: "RBI circular · 20 Sep 2019".
- Series: "Bank aapko paisa dega" (Part 1, 2, 3... RBI compensation rules) brings viewers back daily; end each part
  with the next part's teaser.
- Cover: the ₹ number big + 2-4 words ("₹100 ROZ", "₹5,000 ROZ"), kicker "RBI RULE" / "SCAM ALERT" / "GALTI MAT KARNA".
- Caption: the hook line, 2 lines of what to do, "Source: <rule, date>", "Save karo, kaam aayega 📌", the DM line, then
  4-6 hashtags: #personalfinance #financeinhindi #moneytips + 2-3 topic tags (#rbi #upi #creditscore #gold #scamalert
  #cibil #homeloan). The footer "Education only, not financial advice." is added automatically.
- Voice: a confident, friendly young explainer talking to a friend; clear with numbers; never preachy.
- Characters in scenarios: Mehul and friends (Raghav, Aditya, Shobhit, Shwetal, Saumya, Suhail, Jahnvi, Masood, Animesh,
  Jayesh, Aaryan, Dhruv, Mudit, Shruti), rotating. A scammer gets a made-up name, never a friend's.

## Hard rules
- SEBI (finfluencer rules, Aug 2024; Jan 2025 clarification): education only. Never tell anyone to buy or sell a
  specific stock, fund, coin or policy; no "best fund", price targets or return claims; no stock or market prices from
  the last 3 months; no live market commentary. Brokers and fund houses may only work with us while this holds.
- Every rule names who set it and when (RBI circular + date, NPCI, IRDAI, Income Tax Dept, EPFO, BIS). Every number is
  computed exactly (the numbers check in engine/plan.py) or comes from that source. Forecasts and proposals are said
  as forecasts / proposals ("Goldman Sachs ka anumaan hai"), never as fact.
- Scam reels show how the scam works and how to stay safe, never a method someone could copy.
- Never: on-screen AI label, a fake screenshot of a real bank or app with made-up numbers, an unsourced loss figure.
