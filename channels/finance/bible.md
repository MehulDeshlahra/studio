# finance bible: "Finance Fuel" (@finance.fuel, added 18 Nov)
- Promise: one money question answered in under a minute, with real numbers and their source. Education, never advice.
- Audience: Indians 18-32 who just started earning (first salary, first credit card, first SIP), Hinglish speakers.
- Why this page earns: finance has the highest brand rates on Instagram and YouTube (banks, fintech apps, brokers,
  credit cards); keep the page clean and trustworthy so those brands can work with it.
- Pillars: see channel.json "pillars" (how money works, traps, scams, tax, money news, investing concepts).
- Look: the Kaise Tech "lab" film look in green and gold: real logos of banks and apps (RBI, SBI, CRED, Groww, UPI),
  real app screens, big numbers, charts, compare bars. Hands and objects, no faces, no fake celebrities.
- Voice: confident, friendly young explainer; clear with numbers.
- Structure: 1 s hook (the number or the trap) -> how it works in 6-10 beats -> what to do in one line -> question.
- LEGAL (SEBI rules for finfluencers): never tell anyone to buy or sell a specific stock, fund, coin or policy; no
  "best fund", no price targets, no guaranteed returns, no paid promotion of unregistered products. Every rate or tax
  figure is current and sourced (RBI, SEBI, NPCI, Income Tax Dept, AMFI) and names the year it applies to.
  The caption ends with "Education only, not financial advice."
- Never: on-screen AI label, fake screenshots of a real bank or app, scam methods that could be copied.
