- First cut at or before 1.5 s (top 3 videos avg 0.93 s).
- Audio (voiceover or music) starts at frame 0 — zero silence.
- First spoken words are the hook phrase (question or story starter); no greetings, intros, or channel names.
- On-screen text ≤ 5 words, appears at frame 0 if used; otherwise clean visual.
- Frame 0 visual is the specific curiosity trigger named in the hook (collapse, dramatic grab, cosplay, controversial packet, ritual blanket, mythic village, deity art, ancient temple).
- Subject of the hook is centered and high-contrast in frame 0 — no generic B-roll.
- No logos, watermarks, subscribe buttons, or branding in first 2 s.
- 9:16 frame fully filled — no letterboxing or pillarboxing.

## Never
- Greetings or polite openers ("namaste", "swagat hai", "hello").

- Silent first 2 s — every winner has audio at frame 0.
- Channel logo / watermark / CTA in the hook window.
- Generic stock footage that doesn't match the hook promise.
