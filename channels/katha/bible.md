# katha bible: "Puraan Ki Katha" (@puraankikatha, free on 16 Nov)
- Promise: one story from the Itihasa / Puranas in under 75 seconds, told simply, with the lesson it carries today.
- Sources: Valmiki Ramayana, Mahabharata (critical edition), Bhagavata Purana, Shiva Purana, Panchatantra, Jataka.
  Every reel names its source on screen at the end ("Sroat: Mahabharata, Vana Parva"). If versions differ, say so.
- Look: painterly, cinematic Indian miniature-meets-epic style, warm gold and deep maroon, soft light, ornate but
  uncluttered, 9:16, slow camera moves. Deities and sages drawn with respect: calm faces, traditional attire,
  no distortion, no mockery, no sexualisation.
- Voice: Gemini, warm Hindi storyteller ("dadi-nani" warmth with a modern pace), clear pronunciation of names.
- Structure: 1 s hook (the surprising moment or question) -> the story in 6-10 beats -> the lesson in one line ->
  question to the viewer ("Aap hote toh kya karte?").
- Rules: respectful, no political angle, no caste or religion bashing, no miracles claimed as history,
  no medical/astrological advice.
- Never: on-screen AI label.
