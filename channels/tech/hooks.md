# What the top faceless tech Shorts do in the first 2 seconds (2026-11-18, 8 videos watched)

Top Hindi tech Shorts open with a colloquial Hindi hook word at frame 0, paired with a moving 3D hero asset and a signature SFX, then cut within 1 second. They never waste frames on greetings, branding, 2D art, or silent buildup.

## Rules for our first 2 seconds (obey)
- First spoken word is the Hindi hook keyword (subject noun or question starter), zero greetings or filler; voiceover begins at frame 0.
- On-screen text ≤ 3 words, only if it is the exact hook phrase; appears at frame 0 and disappears by first cut.
- First visual is a high-fidelity 3D asset of the core subject (weapon, vehicle, structure, mechanism) in motion or about to move; no 2D/cartoon for tech topics.
- First cut ≤ 1.0 s for question/myth-bust hooks; ≤ 1.5 s for story/mystery hooks; camera or object motion triggers the cut.
- Audio: signature sound effect (gunshot, whoosh, engine, explosion) at frame 0, instantly followed by voiceover; no music-only intro.
- Zero branding, channel name, or title card in frames 0–2 s.
- Hook sentence completes within first 2 s; grammar is colloquial Hindi (Hinglish allowed), present or past tense, no conditional politeness.
- Visual demonstrates the central mechanism or conflict immediately (bullet firing, bridge panning, shell rotating, missile approaching).

## Never
- Greeting or polite opener (namaste, hello, swagat hai, dosto).
- Static title card, logo, or channel name before the hook visual.
- 2D/cartoon animation for core tech/military/engineering subjects.
- Silence or music-only start; voiceover and SFX must hit at frame 0.
- First cut later than 1.5 s on high-stakes visual hooks (weapons, vehicles, explosions).
