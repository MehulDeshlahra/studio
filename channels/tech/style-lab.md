## Rules
1. Dark lab world: #07080b base, one soft radial glow per beat (warm gold or cool cyan), static fine grain, vignette.
2. Hero per beat is a REAL-looking object (photoreal studio render on pure black, blended with `lighten`), a big
   number, a UI card, a chart, a flow diagram, or a comparison. Never cartoons with faces.
3. Chrome: mono HUD top-left "KAISE.TECH · <series>", top-right pill with the beat's topic; tiny label pill above the
   hero ("UPI · 2016", "PROCESSOR"). Mono for chrome (JetBrains Mono), Inter Tight / Inter for type.
4. Captions: one short sentence chunk (<= 6 words) in sentence case, Inter 700, 56 px, spoken words light up from 35%
   to 100%, ONE keyword in the accent. No yellow boxes, no ALL CAPS shouting.
5. Motion: camera breathes (scale 1.0 -> 1.03) on every beat; heroes enter 0.7-1.0 s power4.out and leave fast;
   numbers count up; bars grow; lines draw; dots travel along paths; elements overlap across cuts (no slide decks).
6. Sound: soft UI ticks, low whooshes, a subtle riser into the payoff; music low.
7. One hook line in the first second, payoff + question at the end, end card with the handle.
