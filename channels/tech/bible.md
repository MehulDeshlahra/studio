# tech bible: "Kaise Hota Hai?"
- Promise: every reel answers ONE "ye kaise hota hai?" question about everyday tech in under a minute, so simply that
  you can explain it to a friend afterwards.
- Look ("lab", channels/tech/style-lab.md): dark premium studio, photoreal product renders on black, big numbers, UI cards,
  flows and charts drawn in code, mono HUD chrome, yellow accent. No cartoons, no characters with eyes.
- REAL brands, always (owner 18 Nov): when the reel is about a named app, product or company (ChatGPT, Google, WhatsApp,
  UPI, PhonePe, iPhone, Jio...), show its REAL logo, and the real product or app screen where it helps. At least one real
  logo in every reel that names a brand; 2-5 real logos/photos per reel is normal. Real photos of public figures (CEOs,
  founders) are fine when the line is about them.
- Voice: Gemini Charon, curious and warm, like explaining to a friend; surprise at the reveal.
- Sound: whoosh on every scene change, pop on titles, click on diagram steps, soft electronic bed.
- Ending: one short payoff line + "Comment mein batao..." question that is easy to answer.
- Never: an on-screen AI label, prices or specs that change, invented numbers.
