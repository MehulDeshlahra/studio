# tech bible: "Kaise Hota Hai?"
- Promise: every reel answers ONE "ye kaise hota hai?" question about everyday tech in under a minute, so simply that
  you can explain it to a friend afterwards.
- Look: glossy 3D Pixar-style objects with big expressive eyes, dark navy studio, soft blue rim light, yellow accent.
  The topic's own object is the hero (chip, battery, SIM card, router, bank server, satellite). No fixed mascot.
- Diagrams: navy cards with blue borders, yellow arrows and a yellow dot that travels the path (flow scenes).
- Voice: Gemini Charon, curious and warm, like explaining to a friend; surprise at the reveal.
- Sound: whoosh on every scene change, pop on titles, click on diagram steps, soft electronic bed.
- Ending: one short payoff line + "Comment mein batao..." question that is easy to answer.
- Never: on-screen AI label, prices, specs, invented numbers, brand logos, real people.
