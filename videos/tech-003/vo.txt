Phone ulta kiya... aur screen khud ghoom gayi. Par phone ko pata kaise chala?
Jaadu nahi hai. Aapke phone ke andar ek chhota sa chip hai. Naam hai... accelerometer.
Is chip ke andar ek bahut hi chhota sa wazan hai, jo patli patli springs pe latka hota hai.
Jaise hi aap phone terha karte ho, gravity us wazan ko neeche kheenchti hai, aur woh thoda sa khisak jaata hai.
Wazan ke dono taraf metal ki plates hain. Woh khiska, plates ke beech ka gap badla, aur chip ne turant naap liya ki neeche kis taraf hai.
Phone samajh gaya ki aapne use terha pakda hai, aur screen ghuma di. Ek second se bhi kam mein.
Aur mazedaar baat? Yahi chip aapke kadam bhi ginta hai. Agla sawaal aap batao, comment mein.
